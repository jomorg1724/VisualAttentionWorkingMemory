# One implementation review

Reviewed the actual `model.py`, `train.py`, `sweep.py`, `README.md`, saved CPU `model_checks.json`, and their inherited SpatialComparison model, schedule and stimulus paths. No substantive issue prevents the authorized production run. This review adds no tests or launch requirements.

- **Teaching is unchanged.** Both arms import the existing SpatialStream and retain its images, cues, classes, heads, comparator and cross-entropy objective. The same seeded 80-update cycle gives nine updates to each of eight orientation/binding cells and four to each motion anchor. There is no angle regression, new cue, auxiliary loss or phase-dependent training rule.
- **The circuit is causal and differentiable.** Previous controller rates supply additive spatial current; current sensory H and previous spatial R supply the next controller state. Both rate/adaptation systems remain connected across all frames. Sensory checkpointing preserves the gradient path. The classifier retains exactly its previous sensory, spatial-memory and old-memory/current-field comparison routes; controller state is not read directly.
- **Source signs are consistent.** Positive recurrent/feedback magnitudes and positive spatial bases carry fixed presynaptic 26E/6I controller signs. The 0.01 initial coupling is a learned constant strength, not a frame-dependent gate. Full-on initialization slightly perturbs the parent (saved maximum logit difference 0.001175); saved checks report exact ordinary-parent and all-feedback-off identity, 111 exact inherited Adam states and nonzero early spatial/controller gradients.
- **Continuation and comparison are coherent.** Compatible moments transfer by parameter name; new controller parameters start fresh. Both arms restore the full parent stream and Python/NumPy/Torch/CUDA RNG states, use global schedule offset 4400 and restore counters by construction rather than adding them twice. Existing learning rates, clipping and decay rules persist. Discarded profiles do not initialize production. The researcher reports the profile-pinned allocation of 4,000 additional updates / 32,000 episodes per arm, with global looks 5400/6400/7400/8400 under the original 14,400-second ledger.
- **The intervention is confined to evaluation.** D24 blank indices are 3–26 for orientation/binding and 10–33 for motion. Samples, query/probe and report computation remain present. Training rejects a supplied intervention mask. Saved evaluation uses the same restored evidence stream across delays and arms, and the same test seed for normal and interrupted feedback. Four-case binding dependence must remain reflected in the planned block bootstrap.

The comparison asks whether this additional recurrent feedback circuit improves the existing tasks under equal additional exposure. It does not isolate attention from added controller parameters/state or content maintenance: H and R both enter the controller, and its 64 additional state scalars can preserve information through feedback. Acute blank-only interruption is outside training and can demonstrate reliance on that pathway, not uniquely a biological attention mechanism or prior erasure. The unchanged 90/10 allocation and primary-only checkpoint selection also remain relevant when interpreting motion trade-offs.

## Completed-result interpretation

Read the final `report.md` and relevant `analysis.json` results. Their conclusion is supported: a narrow single-item D24 improvement (64.26% versus 58.40%; +5.86 pp, paired 95% CI 2.15–9.77) accompanies costs on single-item D12 and motion. This is not a general upgrade. Ordinary continuation itself improves D24 over the parent, so the full parent-to-feedback gain cannot be credited to the added circuit.

Blank-only interruption yields a small single-D24 accuracy difference (−0.20 pp, CI −1.17 to +0.78) and slightly higher AUC, with unchanged binding/motion decisions. This provides no support for an improvement that requires ongoing controller current into R during the blank interval on these episodes. It does not prove that the controller is irrelevant: its state continues evolving, feedback resumes after blanks, and training or encoding/probe interactions can still matter. Chance motion argmax with AUC 0.734 is not an information-erasure result. Intervals are conditional on these trained checkpoints and preserve paired/block structure; they do not measure variability across training seeds. No further computation or source changes were requested or performed for this final interpretation check.
