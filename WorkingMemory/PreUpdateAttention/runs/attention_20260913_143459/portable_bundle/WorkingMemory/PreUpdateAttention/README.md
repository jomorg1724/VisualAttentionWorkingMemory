## Evidence and interpretation

[Santoro et al., Relational recurrent neural networks (2018)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.01822) supplies the joint multihead recurrent-memory interaction precedent; its full design uses gating, which this competitor does not inherit. [Panichello and Buschman (2021)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-021-03390-w) supports internal selection and transformation of remembered representations with shared prefrontal control mechanisms. It does not establish dot-product heads as a biological maintenance circuit. This is a computational mechanism comparison, not evidence that attention is necessary or sufficient for biological working memory.
