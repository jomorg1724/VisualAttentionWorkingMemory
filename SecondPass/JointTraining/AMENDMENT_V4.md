## Fixed-exposure feasibility, not an optimization of the target

The planner replays the exact inherited scheduler and condition queues for all 2,145 future updates. Per-cell optimizer costs use every available v3 optimizer timing, grouped by all 35 cells. Evaluation estimates pool per-episode cell costs across every completed v3 validation and, once available, final evaluation; only timing/coverage fields are used, never final scores. A deduplicated final contributes one timing observation. The allocation includes four validations, two finals, **15% optimizer safety margin**, and **600 seconds startup/checkpoint/report overhead**. Fit is recomputed after predecessor completion; if it exceeds 28,800 seconds, the queue blocks before activation instead of reducing exposure or renewing the cap.

The completed-4-validation preview estimates **28,628.196 seconds** (21,182.040 optimizer seconds before its 15% margin, 3,668.849 evaluation seconds and 600 overhead), leaving **171.804 seconds beyond the specified margin/overhead**. This is an empirical estimate, not a guaranteed finish time. Sensitivity using the maximum observed per-cell evaluation cost rather than the pooled mean is **28,828.459 seconds**, exceeding the cap by 28.459 seconds. Both are retained in timing receipts: the mean method follows v3's empirical-mean allocation approach; the maximum-cost sensitivity is not concealed. Newly completed final timings may change feasibility and can block launch. There is no disposable GPU profile.
