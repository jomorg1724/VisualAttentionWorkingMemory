primary empirical neuroscience

Covert spatial selection in primate basal ganglia

Fabrice Arcizet; Richard J. Krauzlis · 2018

Open external source ↗

Used for: orientation ring · orientation cued · krauzlis cued motion

What was verified

Full primary article retrieved directly from PLOS via urllib; Methods and Figure 1 text read. Firecrawl extraction failed at the service layer; this is not an article-access limitation.

Supporting section / figure

Materials and methods: Motion-direction CD task; Figure 1A; Results: Visual motion-change detection task

Claims supported

  • A peripheral ring marks the relevant patch; target direction changes require a response whereas foil changes and catches do not.
  • Two patch means differ by 90°; 16° Gaussian angular SD; 10-frame/100-ms lifetime; 15°/s; aperture radius 3–3.75° and median 3.25°; mean eccentricity 12°.
  • Primary timing is 0.2-s cue, 0.5-s gap, event at 1.0–4.3 s after motion onset, and response window of 1 s. Event mixture is 57/29/14.
  • 26°/28° are monkey-specific median threshold-adjusted changes, not the complete original stimulus distribution.

Not inherited by this assay

  • The 100×100 raster, 16-dot count, bilinear splats, exact birth law, randomly interleaved target side, and shortened final-report protocol are project adaptations.
  • Does not validate the four-Gabor ring task or plus/minus signed-orientation rule.
  • Does not establish any biological mechanism or learned model performance in this atlas.
