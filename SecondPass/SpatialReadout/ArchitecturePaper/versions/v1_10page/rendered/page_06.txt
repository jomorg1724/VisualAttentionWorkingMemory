The ﬁrst 64 channels of the gate output are write, and the next 64 are reset. A large wt replaces old state with the
candidate; it is not the retention coeﬃcient. Reset is applied to the old hidden ﬁeld before the candidate convolution.
Both convolutions have kernel 3, padding 1, stride 1. The gate convolution maps 128 →128 channels; the candidate
maps 128 →64.[S1]
Gated recurrent units provide learned retention and update paths [2]; convolutional variants replace dense spatial
connectivity with spatially shared kernels [3]. Those precedents motivate the operation, but the local source is the
authority for this particular reset placement and write convention. There is no separate state-to-encoder feedback
connection. The ﬁnal hidden ﬁeld inﬂuences its own next update, not the upstream CNN or KDA modules.
