Retention, initialization, and the size of memory
Reader question: does a retention gate of 0.9 specify a memory lifetime? No. At initialization, the packed
convolution’s gate weights are explicitly zero. The eight retention biases per head are log 9 and the write bias is zero,
so α = 0.9 and β = 0.5 everywhere. Unlike the other packed channels, these initial gates are input-independent.

9.2
A parameter is not a stored state entry
A convolution’s learned kernel is shared across sites and time. Its parameter count therefore does not grow with
the number of sites visited. In contrast, each site/head has its own episode-speciﬁc 8 × 16 matrix. Two heads store
2 · 8 · 16 = 256 scalars per site:

These site-indexed states are not isolated pixels: packed convolutions mix neighborhoods,
GroupNorm mixes spatial statistics, and lower-scale emissions feed deeper memories. None identiﬁes a biological
neuron or synapse.
Takeaway. The update constrains how a past contribution is carried, but input-dependent correction and new
writes prevent reading a behavioral lifetime directly from the initial gate.
