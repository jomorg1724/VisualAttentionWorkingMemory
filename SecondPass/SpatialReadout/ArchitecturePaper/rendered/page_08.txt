In the worked example, c1,0 = −3/50 and c1,1 = 2/5, giving (−3/50)(2, 4)T + (2/5)(2, 0)T = (17/25, −6/25)T. A
negative coeﬃcient is legal, and these coeﬃcients do not sum to one. They are not attention probabilities over

Takeaway. Transport is just the product of the transitions that a past write encounters. The coeﬃcients explain
a realized local read, not a full counterfactual observer.
