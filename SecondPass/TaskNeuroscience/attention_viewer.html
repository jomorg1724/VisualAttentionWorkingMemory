How to read these plots

At every frame the model distributes a total attention probability of one over 100 image patches. A map cell shows the probability for that patch. A regional line adds the probabilities inside its stated region. “Other regions (mean)” averages the individual alternatives; it is not their combined total. The exact masks, trial membership, numerical values and uncertainty intervals are supplied alongside each paper.

Time zero on the line plots is probe, search or change onset. For the motion-duration task it is the first motion transition. The replay slider always shows original frame numbers, including the sample and blank periods.

For image recognition and full-field stimuli, spatial averages describe allocation across image coordinates. The single-trial overlay shows the actual image and its corresponding attention.
