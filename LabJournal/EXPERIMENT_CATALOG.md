# Preserved experiment catalog

[Journal index](README.md) · [Research history](RESEARCH_HISTORY.md) · [Current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md) · [Chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md)

Evidence cutoff: **October 4, 2026**, after the completed angular-contrastive experiment. This catalog consolidates the surviving post-reset repository and its documented experiments; it does not recover deleted pre-reset implementations or results. The numbered journal was reconstructed from preserved sources on September 14 and subsequently extended. That reconstruction date is not the execution date of every earlier experiment.

**Reading the records.** “Fresh” means a new whole learned model unless explicitly qualified; a fresh head on a pretrained encoder is a transfer experiment. “Continuation” preserves trained state; a new architecture does not make inherited weights fresh. An update, a unique generated movie, and a replayed presentation are different units. Four-way motion chance BA is 25%; binary chance BA is 50%. Validation snapshots and validation-selected checkpoints are distinguished from fresh final tests. CPU pathway checks, a running process, fixed-scene memorization and falling training loss do not establish held-out acquisition. Selection remains validation-only; a better terminal test score never retrospectively replaces the selected model.

Many historical checkpoint files were deleted during the explicitly authorized October 3 disk cleanup. Their surviving reports, predictions, manifests and source retain scientific evidence, but a historical checkpoint link does not guarantee that its weights remain available. Each linked journal/report gives the detailed protocol and artifact paths.
