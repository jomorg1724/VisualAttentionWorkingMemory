# Research history of the preserved project

[Journal index](README.md) · [Experiment catalog](EXPERIMENT_CATALOG.md) · [Chronology](CHRONOLOGY.md) · [Current status](CURRENT_STATUS.md)

This account covers the surviving post-reset project through **October 4, 2026**. It explains the sequence of research questions and decisions; the [catalog](EXPERIMENT_CATALOG.md) records individual exposures, endpoints and evidence gaps. It does not restore deleted pre-reset code or turn old plans into experiments. Historical journal entries sometimes retain “running” or “not launched” wording: their dates and actual run reports determine what happened, not their present tense.

The project began with pre-attentive vision as one component of a larger visual-attention/working-memory system. Guided Search6.0 provided a functional scaffold, with its diffuser omitted and activated long-term memory approximated by synaptic weights. Those choices are modeling assumptions, not claims that the completed neural models implement Wolfe's theory or monkey neuroscience.

## Sensory discrimination came before working memory

The first preserved comparison examined five lightweight convolutional encoders using ordered image pairs. The user corrected an initial static/binary-dot objective to four-way motion direction, so the superseded attempt cannot serve as evidence about the corrected task. Seven tasks then covered motion, signed orientation, contrast, spatial frequency, chromatic increment, contour grouping and natural-image spectral detail. At756updates/model, no encoder dominated: ConvNeXt had useful motion performance, while other encoders did better on contours. A saved-model ensemble modestly helped contours but cost motion; because the test had already been inspected, that audit was exploratory. [Encoder screen](experiments/01-pav-encoder-screen.md), [ensemble audit](experiments/02-saved-ensemble-audit.md).

The next two experiments separated an architectural addition from teaching allocation. Fixed Gabor-contour and learned late-SE additions were **warm continuations** of ConvNeXt. Neither met the prespecified contour gain with motion preservation. In contrast, continuing the late-SE candidate with half the updates on contours moved contour BA from67.9% to96.2%, while all seven focused-arm point estimates exceeded95%. This supported the particular allocation package; it did not identify gradient interference or prove a universal curriculum. [Hybrids](experiments/03-hybrid-continuation.md), [allocation](experiments/04-contour-task-allocation.md).

With the selected2268encoder frozen, three newly learned temporal readouts were compared: spatial KDA, ConvGRU and opponent fast/slow traces with fixed motion-energy computations. The opponent candidate reached98.44–100% across seven tasks, including100% motion. Resetting its history dropped motion to chance, demonstrating use of the ordered pair. This was a two-frame integration success, not evidence about long-delay retention. The original KDA motion result was31.70%; later spatial-KDA curriculum success belongs to a different task and training lineage. [Temporal comparison](experiments/05-causal-temporal-accumulators.md).

## Retention failure was not equivalent to inaccessible information

The broad sequence battery warm-started the opponent winner and unfroze learned weights. Sensory anchors remained strong, but integration/decision and retrospective tasks stayed near chance after39,200episodes. Difficulty with newly introduced rules at short delays meant that this could not be interpreted as a clean memory-capacity failure. [Sequence battery](experiments/06-sequence-battery.md).

A subsequent matched40,000-episode comparison put fresh LSTM or dense E/I recurrent cores behind inherited sensory weights. Both learned useful behavior; L8 motion favored the LSTM79.69% versus E/I68.95%, while delayed orientation was similar. A frozen recency diagnostic found E/I decisions favored late evidence despite matched direction totals and exact final rasters. That established ordering sensitivity, not a uniquely determined adaptation or leakage mechanism. [Recurrent comparison](experiments/07-lstm-versus-ei.md), [recency](experiments/08-recency-diagnostic.md).

The next question was whether the E/I population retained information that its classifier failed to use. Analysis-only probes improved motion decisions from68.65% to78.13%, with early directional counts still accessible at the final state. That finding deferred a proposed slow-synapse change and motivated refitting only the existing deployed output. The readout continuation improved long-motion BA70.31%→79.30% with upstream weights unchanged. Neither result required installing the diagnostic probe as a main-model component. [State access](experiments/09-state-accessibility.md), [readout refit](experiments/10-readout-refit.md).

Training that existing recurrent/output system on inserted blank delays then produced motion BA around80% through24blanks, but orientation comparison remained near chance atD24. Frozen probes found actual sample angle still decodable there with4.25° mean error; a late-trained decoder worked far better than transferring the sample-time decoder, indicating a changed accessible representation. A label-only diagnostic comparator reached73.44% versus deployed50%. This ruled out a blanket inference of erased sample information; it did not establish that the deployed network learned an adequate comparison rule. [Retention](experiments/11-retention-learning.md), [orientation access](experiments/12-orientation-accessibility.md).

## Spatial memory and attention helped particular tasks, with costs

Spatial E/I fields and a learned old-memory/current-sensory comparator next competed with a dense-memory branch. Both inherited sensory weights, and the dense branch also inherited a trained core. Spatial binding atD24 reached93.36% versus dense49.61%, including94.53% at held-out locations. Single-orientation D24 remained weak and motion deteriorated. The spatial branch had42.25times as many persistent state elements, so this was a package comparison, not isolated proof of locality or clean two-item capacity. [Spatial comparison](experiments/13-spatial-memory.md).

An additive32-unit controller was tested against ordinary continuation from the same spatial parent. It gave a modest single-orientation D24 improvement but harmed other conditions. Silencing its blank-period feedback had no clear cost. Proposals to change angle supervision or add cue curricula were explicitly rejected: the authorized comparison changed architecture while preserving teaching. [Selective maintenance](experiments/14-selective-maintenance.md).

Pre-update attention was more useful for delayed orientation. Previous-memory queries attended jointly to visual and memory sources before the E/I update, lifting singleD24 BA58.40%→79.30%; binding stayed at ceiling, while motionD0 worsened. Frozen interventions showed that excluding memory-source keys during blanks removed the orientation gain. Because exclusion also changes normalization and is out of distribution, the experiment did not uniquely distinguish active refreshing from rejecting blank input. Saved predictions additionally revealed severe motion decision bias: a validation-fitted class-offset diagnostic improved D0 decisions but transferred poorly toD24. [Attention](experiments/15-preupdate-attention.md), [interventions](experiments/15a-attention-mechanism.md), [motion audit](experiments/15b-motion-audit.md).

A10% versus50% motion allocation continuation improved selected motion scores, but the focused branch had late collapse and an orientation cost. Equal terminal exposure did not mean equal selected-checkpoint exposure. Actual per-frame attention maps were later visualized without turning source mass or spatial locality into proof of task content. The broader latent PCA/UMAP/t-SNE study was paused before extraction; it has no completed findings. [Allocation comparison](experiments/16-training-exposure.md), [maps](experiments/17-attention-maps.md).

## Repeated native failures prompted audits and fresh baselines

Removing both source/locality attention biases damaged previously acquired orientation/binding. Every validation preservation screen failed, making the selected endpoint the untouched biased parent rather than a successful trained competitor. The separate new five-task arm was cancelled. Restoring those biases and teaching the new battery produced strong binding and some recognition, but duration, orientation and Krauzlis remained weak. Motion-only continuation likewise failed even atD0. These were still warm-start branches, not fresh acquisition experiments. [Bias removal](experiments/18-unbiased-attention-spatial-battery.md), [five tasks](experiments/19-biased-spatial-battery.md), [motion-only](experiments/20-single-task-motion.md).

Prospective sensory queries, spatial priority readout, dual attention and AV-context revisions followed. Several were cancelled before final testing. The initial priority-readout launch incorrectly inherited trained weights; it was stopped and replaced with a verified scratch model. Dual-attention retrieval lost all checkpoints, though partial validation survives. AV-contextv2 preserved a durable10496checkpoint and weak validation, but had no final held-out endpoint. These distinctions matter: an unintended warm launch, an unfinished trial and a lost artifact are not interchangeable demonstrations of architectural failure. The combined architecture lineage was closed on September16. [Prospective query](experiments/21-prospective-query.md), [priority readout](experiments/22-spatial-priority-readout.md), [dual attention](experiments/23-dual-attention-priority.md), [AV context](experiments/24-av-context-v2.md).

The battery audit then checked whether the native renderer, labels, streams and loss paths contained usable evidence. Image-only observers performed well, while a trained model could be nearly input-invariant. The historical Krauzlis observer in this audit used13° changes; it must not be conflated with the later26/28° native task. Bounded gradient and determinism checks reduced particular bug hypotheses without proving that every optimization path was sound or that a neural model should acquire the rule. [Battery audit](experiments/25-battery-audit.md).

Plain fresh CNN/global-GRU baselines also failed direct native acquisition under several recipes. Easier sign/ring orientation tasks learned, and continuing those weights onto native cuedD0 produced near-perfect performance. Adding long delays remained difficult. A staged ring→cuedD0→delay curriculum subsequently let both ConvGRU and spatial KDA reach100% atD24 across two seeds; the plain decoder lagged and the opponent branch was seed-sensitive. This supports acquisition under that staged protocol, not from-scratch native delayed acquisition without curriculum. Cloud checkpoints were lost and the declared provider cap was exceeded; a separately documented local KDA rerun preserved weights and reproduced trained-delay success, while cue jitter/contrast exposed fragility. [Plain baseline](experiments/26-plain-baseline-rung1.md), [accumulator study](experiments/27-accumulator-conv-stack.md).

## SecondPass tested an unchanged joint suite and readout migrations

The SecondPass suite consolidated13tasks/35primaryconditions with native stimulus and official image splits intact. A whole-model-fresh multiscale KDA/global-GRU learner used one Adam1e-4, no clipping, FP32 full BPTT and ordinary labels. The first completed715-update run acquired some sensory distinctions but not spatial memory or Krauzlis. Its worst-task selection criterion chose117, despite terminal715 having a better mean AUC; later selection changed prospectively rather than using test results to rewrite the winner. [Suite assembly](task-suite-assembly.md), [joint run](joint-suite-training.md), [results](../SecondPass/JointTraining/RESULTS.md).

Further exact-state continuations reached2860 and then3393. A new final spatial ConvGRU branch inherited the trained CNN, all three KDAs and compatible optimizer/stream state while initializing its changed readout. The first1690-update branch and additional5070updates completed with full35-cell final tests. Sensory motion eventually reached100%, but spatial selection, duration and Krauzlis remained weak. There was no exposure-matched continuing global-GRU control; success or failure cannot be causally assigned solely to the ConvGRU replacement. [Migration and results](spatial-readout-convgru.md).

Frozen native probes separated sensory accessibility from deployed decisions. AtD0, cue and sample orientations were accessible. Label-only comparators did not reliably rescue behavior, whereas explicitly auxiliary-supervised structured comparators did. Matched final-state versus time-separated comparisons showed that external stored samples were not necessary for strong early-feature decoding, but compression, capacity and supervision remained confounded across layers. These were analysis-only experiments, not evidence that the unchanged final-label learner acquired spatial comparison. [Failure audit](../SecondPass/SpatialReadout/FailureAnalysis/FINDINGS.md), [features](../SecondPass/SpatialReadout/FeatureDiagnostic/REPORT.md), [temporal access](../SecondPass/SpatialReadout/TemporalAccessDiagnostic/REPORT.md).

A warm spatial-comparison decoder and then warm recurrent-transformer/CLS branch each completed2600addedupdates. Sensory results were useful but spatial tasks stayed weak, with late motion deterioration in the transformer. The next no-CLS conv-decoder trials were fresh, then user-cancelled; an architecture rollback did not authorize loading old weights. [Comparison decoder](../SecondPass/SpatialComparisonReadout/CloudRun/RESULTS.md), [transformer](../SecondPass/SpatialRecurrentTransformer/RESULTS.md), [no-CLS branch](../SecondPass/SpatialRecurrentConvDecoder/README.md), [baseline boundary](../SecondPass/ACTIVE_BASELINE.md).

The user then requested the entire CNN+threeKDAs+spatialConvGRU+head from scratch. That run stopped/saved at7739 on request and continued unchanged under a separately authorized long allowance. Validation28539 showed all seven sensory tasks plus ring, cued orientation and binding at100%, while duration and Krauzlis stayed weak. Frozen checkpoint35039 confirmed sampled native orientation/binding competence, graded smaller-angle behavior and actual spatial activity maps. Exploratory perturbation effects were small and did not establish monkey-equivalent mechanisms. The retained top-level records do not locate a final long-continuation report, so its111739target is not asserted as achieved. A separate whole-fresh terminal spatial-transformer variant also retains launch/prospective-allocation evidence without a located final joint-suite report. ## Native Krauzlis remained a fresh-acquisition problem

A whole-fresh single-task spatial-consolidation model completed4595nativeupdates and147040fresh episodes. Both selected and terminal tests had50%BA in all three conditions, with every response positive. Higher terminal AUC did not replace the selected2297checkpoint. This native task preserves the cue, competing patch, dot lifetime and26/28° changes; binary57% majority accuracy is not acquisition. [Local outcome](krauzlis-fresh-attempt02.md).

Frozen diagnostics asked whether cue, direction, side-of-change and event status could be recovered. Cue remained decodable after the gap, while operational side/event/label probes were weak. Image-only observers did well, but simple neural probes also failed their own raw-pixel positive-control tests. Even a pixel-gated readout family's failure on early CNN features did not uniquely prove that those features erased motion. The sequence of probes therefore narrowed particular explanations without localizing one decisive bottleneck. [State](krauzlis-frozen-diagnostic.md), [factorization](krauzlis-factorized-diagnostic.md), [upstream](krauzlis-upstream-motion-diagnostic.md), [adequacy](krauzlis-probe-adequacy-diagnostic.md), [matched readout](krauzlis-motion-readout-diagnostic.md).

An easier±90° adaptation also had fresh CNN/dense packages and exact deployed-path integrity checks. Their preparation notes and later audit references are temporally inconsistent as summaries; the catalog flags missing bound final reports rather than inventing completion from planned exposure. A conventional learned axial-attention positive control fitted32fixed movies after an optimizer/batch correction, yet its separately fresh19232-trial stream still ended at50%BA. Startup gradients and fixed-set memorization proved pathway/capacity facts, not unseen cue-motion acquisition. [Integrity audit](../SecondPass/SpatialReadout/SpatialConsolidation/KrauzlisFailureAudit/TrainingIntegrityAudit/REPORT.md), [neural control](../SecondPass/SpatialReadout/SpatialConsolidation/KrauzlisFailureAudit/NeuralPositiveControl/REPORT.md).

The whole-sequence KDA experiment then simplified the architecture: all image patches were presented as one ordered sequence with a terminal CLS readout. One2-head KDA completed4216updates/134912fresh native movies at50%BA; three layers were cancelled2615updates with only chance validation; one16-head KDA completed matched4216updates, also50%BA. More layers/heads changed parameter and state capacity, so neither was a clean isolated depth/head-count test. All used full temporal gradients and fresh model/Adam/RNG/streams. [Single layer](krauzlis-sequence-kda-design.md), [depth](krauzlis-sequence-kda3-depth.md), [sixteen heads](krauzlis-sequence-kda16-heads.md).

Independent current/previous CNN encoders with a dense GRU were also fresh and cancelled after2575updates. An original ordered-two-frame CNN/RViT learner was cancelled2075updates. Replacing it with another fresh model and1000-movie pools replayed10epochs lowered within-pool loss but completed2310updates with selected50%BA and terminal47.83%BA on new movies. Adding a fixed motion-energy frontend completed660updates near chance. Combining that frontend with a learned identity memory carry and random-frame parameter-gradient estimator completed3960updates at50%BA. The combined policy's negative result does not identify either component as the cause. [Delayed CNN/GRU](krauzlis-delayed-frame-gru.md), [online RViT](krauzlis-two-frame-rvit.md), [replay](krauzlis-rvit-replay.md), [motion frontend](krauzlis-simoncelli-heeger.md), [sampled gradients](krauzlis-random-frame-gradients.md).

The training-path audit reproduced expected objective and microbatch behavior, including partial replay tails. Very small early recurrent gradients in particular RViT examples contrasted with a healthier KDA16 gradient ratio, but no causal conditioning intervention was run. Gradient presence, saturation, temporal attenuation and learning-to-criterion are separate questions. [Audit](krauzlis-training-path-audit.md).

## Simplifying stimuli and learning reconstruction changed the question

The next task removed the cue and competing patch, retaining the target patch's pixels, timing and native change angles. The original RViT architecture on this **cue-free modification** was cancelled at logged1726/durable1700. Weighted raw-frame integration (.5current+.4previous+.1two-back, startup repeats first) fed a fresh CNN/GRU; that local run was cancelled570updates. These are partial attempts, not completed native attention tests. [Single stimulus](krauzlis-single-stimulus.md), [weighted CNN/GRU](krauzlis-weighted-mean-conv-gru.md).

A fresh convolutional VAE instead learned three-frame reconstruction from active windows, without response labels. Its spatial Gaussian latent was256×13×13; foreground/background-balanced loss prevented a nearly empty image from making constant gray look successful. Every replay presentation drew a new active window. The run completed9900updates/300000triplets/30000unique movies after a disk-full save failure and exact5600recovery. The original cap and target were retained;100unsavedupdates/3032presentations were physically repeated and disclosed. Prior best5500weights had been deleted, so eligible selection restarted from recovered5600rather than pretending the old winner remained available. Final balanced reconstruction and temporal-difference errors beat copy-middle, although full-frame MSE was roughly tied. No response classification was learned by that objective. [VAE journal](krauzlis-three-frame-vae.md), [final reconstruction report](../SecondPass/ThreeFrameConvVAE/FINAL_REPORT.md).

The latest9900encoder and mean head then initialized a new RViT classifier, with new recurrence/readout and fresh classification Adam/RNG/streams; all transferred weights remained trainable. This was explicitly **pretrained transfer**, not whole-model fresh. The initial run was cancelled227updates. A matched new classifier amplified tokens tenfold before the RViT and completed330updates at50%BA. Internal normalization means that this scaling is not simply changing attention temperature. [Transfer](vae-encoder-rvit.md), [input scaling](vae-rvit-input10.md).

A whole-fresh weighted-mean CNN/RViT followed without any VAE checkpoint. Its10-epoch cloud run was replaced at1304updates; a new whole-fresh run changed only replay10→2epochs and completed2310updates/70000presentations/35000unique movies. Selected and terminal BA remained50%. The fivefold increase in unique movies is clear, but the10-epoch predecessor was cancelled at lower exposure, so this is not a completed matched-endpoint sampling comparison. [Ten epochs](weighted-mean-rvit.md), [two epochs](weighted-mean-rvit-epoch2.md).

## Prediction and explicit angular supervision produced different evidence

A new synthetic distribution made future motion predictable: persistent full-field Gaussian dots, all sharing one continuous direction and speed, with periodic wrapping. There were no cues, distractors, limited lifetimes or change labels. A fresh variational CNN encoded three frames into one512D Gaussian vector and predicted the fourth. The20minute pilot was blurry and worse than copy-last under balanced error. The user then authorized substantial same-state continuation, which completed50240cumulativeupdates/1.57millionpresentations/785000unique samples. On768fresh sequences, balanced predictionMSE.00029275 was92.4% below copy-last and improved at all three speeds. That establishes prediction competence on this distribution, not a native cue-selected decision or a uniquely identified direction representation. [Predictor history](variational-motion-prediction.md), [final report](../SecondPass/VariationalMotionPredictor/FINAL_REPORT.md).

Freezing the best50000predictive encoder and training a small FFN on concatenated before/after512Dmean embeddings did not yield change detection. All10240headupdates completed; fresh3072comparison tests had50%BA/AUC.49519 and every decision was no-change. This negative result concerns the frozen representation and tested head recipe. It does not prove absent information or report an unperformed end-to-end finetuning experiment. [Frozen head experiment](predictive-motion-change.md), [final report](../SecondPass/PredictiveMotionChange/FINAL_REPORT.md).

The latest experiment trained a **whole-fresh CNN** with explicit simulator-angle supervision. A128Dunit embedding learned pair distances proportional to shortest angular separation; layouts, counts and speeds differed independently across clips. After25024updates/782000pairpresentations, a validation-fitted distance threshold achieved100%BA/AUC1 on3072fresh comparison presentations, representing1536paired nuisance contexts. The angular test had loss.00136432 and correlation.987835; best19000/latest25024were verified with all64Adam states. This is a positive result for one explicitly supervised representation and one simplified synthetic distribution. It is not a controlled ablation of supervision alone, because architecture, loss and training exposure also differ from the frozen predictive-head experiment. [Contrastive journal](angular-contrastive-motion.md), [final result](../SecondPass/AngularContrastiveMotion/FINAL_REPORT.md), [technical report](../SecondPass/AngularContrastiveMotion/TECHNICAL_REPORT.md).

The unresolved transfer question is whether this angular representation supports the original selected-stimulus decision with native cue, distractor and dot-lifetime properties. That work has not been performed. Earlier memory successes, curriculum successes, reconstruction/prediction gains and the new angular result answer different questions; none silently replaces the repeated native Krauzlis failures or authorizes another run.
